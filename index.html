Value of CEBS Certification

The CEBS certification is widely regarded as the premier designation within the benefits industry. The curriculum for group plan benefits and retirement plan administration is meticulously developed in collaboration with the Dalhousie University. Successfully passing the five comprehensive exams signifies a deep level of expertise in each subject area.

The CEBS certification connects professionals with a network of peers, alumni, and industry experts, offering endless opportunities for career development, knowledge sharing, and staying updated on industry trends.
