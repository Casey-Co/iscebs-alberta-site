A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Membership questions

Can I join while studying for CEBS?+
Yes. Active CEBS students are eligible for Society and local chapter membership. Review the current eligibility details on the official ISCEBS joining page.
Is chapter membership the same as Society membership?+
The Alberta Chapter is your local community within ISCEBS. Add chapter membership when you join or renew with the Society.
What does membership cost?+
Society and chapter dues are listed in the official joining process. Follow the Join Us Today link to see current rates before applying.
Who can help me get started?+
Email ab.iscebs@gmail.com with your questions about the Alberta Chapter.
