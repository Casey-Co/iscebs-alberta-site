Value of CEBS Certification

The CEBS certification is widely regarded as the premier designation within the benefits industry. The curriculum for group plan benefits and retirement plan administration is meticulously developed in collaboration with the Dalhousie University. Successfully passing the five comprehensive exams signifies a deep level of expertise in each subject area.

The CEBS certification connects professionals with a network of peers, alumni, and industry experts, offering endless opportunities for career development, knowledge sharing, and staying updated on industry trends.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

CEBS questions

Do I need to earn both GBA and RPA first?+
The CEBS designation requires all five course exams. The GBA and RPA specialty designations can be earned along the way by completing their specified three-course combinations.
Where do I enrol and book exams?+
Enrolment, course materials and exams are administered through the International Foundation. Use the official Canadian program links on this page.
Can the chapter support my journey?+
You’re welcome in our community while you study. Join chapter education and connect with other benefits and retirement professionals.
Where do I find current fees and requirements?+
Visit the official Canadian CEBS program for the latest course, exam and enrolment information.
