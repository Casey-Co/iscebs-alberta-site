FROM THE PRESIDENT

A welcome from Rob

Rob Staniloff
2027 Alberta Chapter President

Proposed message · pending Rob’s approval

Happy New Year, Alberta Chapter members and friends.

As I begin my term as President, I’m looking forward to getting to know more of you and building on the work of our chapter volunteers.

First, congratulations to everyone who passed an exam or completed a designation between October and December. Making time to study alongside work, family and everything else takes commitment. Whether you passed your first course or finished your CEBS, you have something to be proud of.

We want this newsletter to be a place to celebrate those milestones, share what’s coming up and introduce the people in our chapter.

Our board is planning education and opportunities to connect throughout the year. We’d like to hear which topics would be useful in your work—and what would make it easier for you to take part.

Thank you for being part of the Alberta Chapter. I look forward to learning with you in 2027.

Rob

BOARD MEMBER SPOTLIGHT

Meet Alex Maleki

RPA, LLQP · 2027 Chapter Secretary

Account Manager, Group Retirement Services
HUB International Insurance Brokers

Connect with Alex ↗

From Toronto to Alberta

Alex brings experience from the Toronto ISCEBS chapter to our Alberta board, along with a background in group retirement and client service.

He earned his RPA designation in September 2024. Later that year, he attended Toronto’s CEBS Graduate Recognition event, connecting with fellow professionals pursuing education in group benefits and retirement.

Now based in Calgary, Alex works as an Account Manager at HUB International and brings that perspective to our chapter community.
