US CEBS PROGRAM

Did you know?

Some of our board members are US CEBS graduates. If you are visiting our website from the United States or are interested in the US curriculum, we can readily provide expert guidance on the US program. We are proud to be one of the few chapters in Canada capable of offering this specialized support. Please reach out to us by email if you have any questions.
